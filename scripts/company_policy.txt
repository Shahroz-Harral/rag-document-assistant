# Acme Corp — Internal Employee Handbook & Policy Guide

## 1. Working Hours & Remote Work
- Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST, Monday through Friday.
- All full-time employees are eligible for a hybrid work schedule (up to 3 days remote per week).
- Remote Work Allowance: Employees receive a one-time stipend of $500 to setup their home office, plus a $50/month internet reimbursement.

## 2. Refund & Return Policy
- Customer refunds are valid within 30 days of original purchase.
- All returns require a valid proof of purchase or order ID.
- Digital downloads and custom software development services are non-refundable after 14 days of delivery.

## 3. IT Support & Contact Information
- For password resets, software licenses, or hardware issues, contact IT Helpdesk at support@acmecorp-example.com or call +1-800-555-0199.
- Critical system outages should be reported directly to emergency-it@acmecorp-example.com.

## 4. Vacation & Paid Time Off (PTO)
- Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per calendar year.
- Unused PTO up to 5 days can be carried over into the next year.
- Employees must submit PTO requests at least 2 weeks in advance via the HR portal.
